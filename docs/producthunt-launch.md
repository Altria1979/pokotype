## Maker first comment

Hi Product Hunt! 👋

Pokotype is a browser-based Japanese typing practice app that takes you from individual kana to full articles.

Start with hiragana or katakana, choose the rows you want to practice, and type their romaji. Alternative spellings are supported, and completed sessions help you revisit difficult kana. When you're ready, practice original articles sentence by sentence, in groups, or as a full text, with reading hints and Japanese speech playback.

The core exercises are free and require no account. Optional AI generation creates articles by topic and N5–N1 level using your own DeepSeek or Alibaba Cloud Model Studio API key; provider usage costs may apply. Articles and completed practice records stay in your browser.

I'd love feedback on the typing experience and which practice features would help you most!
